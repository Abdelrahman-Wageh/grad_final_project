## 🎯 How to Use Friend Mode

### Step 1: Navigate to Friend Tab
Click the 3rd tab (💬 صاحبي) in the bottom navigation

### Step 2: Press Microphone
Press and hold the microphone button at the bottom

### Step 3: Speak
Speak your message in Arabic or English

### Step 4: Release
Release the button when done speaking

### Step 5: Watch
- Farfour thinks 🤔
- AI processes (2-3 seconds)
- Farfour speaks 💬
- Audio plays
- Message appears in chat

### Step 6: Continue
Keep chatting! Farfour remembers the conversation.
