# 📚 Smartino User Guide

**Welcome to Smartino (صديقي الذكي)!**

Your child's offline-first educational AI companion for learning through play.

---

## 🌟 What is Smartino?

Smartino is an educational app designed for Egyptian children aged 4-8 that makes learning fun through:
- 🎮 **3 Interactive Games**: Code Commander, Story Weaver, and Potion Shop
- 💬 **AI Friend**: Chat with Smartino using your voice
- ⭐ **Rewards**: Earn stars and unlock treasures
- 📊 **Progress Tracking**: See your achievements grow
- 🎨 **Disney-Quality**: Beautiful, child-friendly design

**Best Part**: Works 100% offline - no internet needed!

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### First Time Setup

1. **Launch the App**
   - Tap the Smartino icon on your device
   - Wait for the splash screen (3 seconds)

2. **Character Selection** (First time only)
   - Choose your favorite character
   - This will be your profile

3. **Main Screen**
   - You'll see 4 tabs at the bottom:
     - 🎮 **ألعاب** (Games)
     - 📚 **فصول** (Chapters)
     - 💬 **صاحبي** (My Friend)
     - ⭐ **لوحتي** (My Dashboard)

---

## 🎮 Games Tab (ألعاب)

### How to Play Games

1. **Tap the Games Tab** (🎮 ألعاب)
2. **Choose a Game**:
   - 🤖 **Code Commander** (قائد الأكواد)
   - 📖 **Story Weaver** (نساج القصص)
   - 🧪 **Potion Shop** (محل الجرعات)
3. **Play and Learn!**

### Game 1: Code Commander 🤖

**What You Learn**: Logic and problem-solving

**How to Play**:
1. Help Smartino reach the battery
2. Drag arrow commands to create a path
3. Press "Execute" to see Smartino move
4. Reach the battery to win!

**Tips**:
- Plan your path before executing
- Avoid obstacles (gray squares)
- Use the fewest commands possible

### Game 2: Story Weaver 📖

**What You Learn**: Vocabulary and creativity

**How to Play**:
1. Listen to the story
2. Fill in the blank word using your voice
3. Press the microphone button
4. Say the missing word
5. Complete the story!

**Tips**:
- Speak clearly into the microphone
- Think creatively - many answers work!
- Listen to the full story

### Game 3: Potion Shop 🧪

**What You Learn**: Math and numbers

**How to Play**:
1. See the target number
2. Drag potions together to mix them
3. Add the numbers to reach the target
4. Create the correct potion!

**Tips**:
- Add the numbers in your head first
- Try different combinations
- Colorful potions are fun to mix!

## 💬 My Friend Tab (صاحبي)

### Talk to Smartino

**What It Does**: Have a conversation with Smartino using your voice!

**How to Use**:
1. **Tap the Friend Tab** (💬 صاحبي)
2. **Press the Microphone Button** (bottom of screen)
3. **Speak Your Message**
   - Say hello!
   - Ask questions
   - Tell stories
   - Share your day
4. **Listen to Smartino's Response**
   - Smartino will talk back to you
   - Have a real conversation!

**Tips**:
- Speak clearly and not too fast
- Wait for Smartino to finish talking
- Be creative with your questions!
- Smartino remembers your conversations

**Privacy**: All conversations stay on your device - 100% private!

## 🎯 Tips for Parents

### How to Help Your Child

1. **First Session**:
   - Sit with your child for the first 10 minutes
   - Show them how to navigate
   - Help them understand the games

2. **Microphone Permissions**:
   - Allow microphone access for voice features
   - This enables Friend Tab and Story Weaver

3. **Volume**:
   - Keep volume at comfortable level
   - Smartino speaks in Arabic and English

4. **Play Time**:
   - Recommended: 20-30 minutes per session
   - Dashboard shows total play time

5. **Progress**:
   - Check Dashboard regularly
   - Celebrate achievements together
   - Encourage consistent play

### Parent Dashboard

**Access**: Tap the parent button (👨‍👩‍👧 لوحة الآباء) on home screen

**PIN**: Default is `1234` (change in settings)

**What You Can See**:
- Detailed progress analytics
- Words learned over time
- Daily play time
- Mastered concepts
- Unlocked items

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem**: Microphone not working  
**Solution**: 
- Check app permissions in device settings
- Allow microphone access
- Restart the app

**Problem**: No sound  
**Solution**:
- Check device volume
- Check app sound settings
- Ensure device is not on silent mode

**Problem**: App is slow  
**Solution**:
- Close other apps
- Restart device
- Ensure enough storage space

**Problem**: Can't see Smartino  
**Solution**:
- Look in top-right corner
- Smartino is a small circle
- Tap it to interact!

**Problem**: Games won't load  
**Solution**:
- Wait a few seconds
- Restart the app
- Check device storage
